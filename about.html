Hi!

My name is Alysa Kulchenko, and I am a 20 year old student at the University of Washington. Daughter to Ukrainian parents, and sister to two older brothers, I’ve lived in Washington my whole life.

Since I can remember, I’ve loved art, especially realism. My fascination with drawing and painting flourished during Covid while I was bored and stuck in quarantine. With nothing to do, I became obsessed with replicating everything around me. My favorite thing to draw was people because of how unique every face is. With practice, I got really good at looking at faces and being able to copy them down on paper. This interest in art helped propel me into the field of digital and web design after I realized that I can combine artistic skills with code.

I have been learning how to program since my sophomore year in high school. One of my first experiences combining coding and visual design was a program I wrote in Python, which was selected to compete in a district-wide computer science contest. The program was a game similar to whack-a-mole, featuring a garden with vegetables and holes in the dirt. Moles would randomly pop out of the holes, and the user had a limited amount of time to knock down as many as possible. I made sure to incorporate my artistic skills to make each vegetable visually appealing and added detailed designs to the moles. This project propelled my interest in combining computer science and visual design.

Aside from art, I have also been an athlete since childhood. Soccer is my main sport but the gym has had my heart in current years. Overall, I have played countless other sports: from basketball to ballet, kayaking to flag football, the range of sports in my arsenal is limitless. I’m lucky to live in Washington as my most recent obsession has been hiking. I’m excited to see what mountains I’ll ascend in the next few months.

Motivation

I created this website to showcase the work that has shaped my portfolio, from my earliest to my most recent. Countless hours have gone into assembling the projects, writing the documentation, and designing the website. My goal for my portfolio is to create a space for my work, highlighting the growth of my skills as this website continues to evolve.

Each of the project docs focuses on a different aspect of my skills. MemoMate dives into my technical and coding experience, the District Market prototype focuses on my eye for visual design, and Dr Michael Woo’s website emphasizes my experience working firsthand with clients. As this portfolio fills with projects that showcase my technical experience, I want to continue to remember and celebrate my art, the original skill that inspired the career path I am on today.
